# WI-01 · Set up a workspace, robots and a recording

**Purpose:** get the three things every other instruction needs: the map the robots use, the list of robots, and a recorded drive through the trouble spot.

**You need:** this PC on the robot network. No password is needed; the robot answers read requests from any PC on its network.

## A. Workspace

A workspace is one folder for one site. It holds `maps`, `candidates`, `drives`, `results` and `robots.json`.

1. Click the workspace path at the top left.
2. Type the full path of a folder, for example `D:\Maps\Springfield`, and press **Use this folder**. Missing sub-folders are created.

## B. Robots

1. In the left panel, next to **Robots**, press **+**.
2. Type one robot per line: name, a space, the IP address. Example: `AMR-03 10.0.0.27`.
3. Press **Add**. Click a robot to read its position, confidence and current map.

## C. The map the robots use now

Always start from the file that is on the robot today. A file from last week may lack edits somebody made on the robot since.

1. Point at the robot in the list and press **map**.
2. The file is copied into `maps` with the robot name, date and time in its name, and shown.

If you only have a file, use **Import map…** at the top.

## D. Record a drive

A recording is what lets the program test any map, including one that was never on a robot.

1. Point at the robot and press **rec**. Give the recording a name and a time limit, then **Start recording**.
2. Drive the robot through the trouble spot: by hand, or let it run its normal task. Start at least 15 m before the spot and continue 15 m past it. A closed loop is best.
3. Click the red **REC** badge at the top to stop. The recording appears under **Recordings**.

> The recorder reads position, confidence and the laser scan about every 0.7 s. It sends no command to the robot. Keep the window open while it records.

## What makes a good recording

- It crosses the place where robots jump, in the direction they normally drive.
- It also covers a long stretch where the map is known to be good. The measurement in [[WI-03]] needs both.
- Normal driving speed. Standing still adds nothing.
- 5 to 15 minutes is enough for one area.
