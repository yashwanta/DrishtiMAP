# WI-05 · Trial on one robot, then the fleet

**Purpose:** prove the candidate on the floor with one robot before every robot depends on it.

**You need:** a candidate that passed [[WI-04]], RoboShop, and one robot you can take out of production.

> DRISHTI Map does not load maps. Steps 2, 3 and 9 are done by a person in RoboShop or the fleet manager.

## Steps

1. Press **Open folder** and find the candidate in `candidates`. Read its `report.txt` once more.
2. In RoboShop, load the candidate on the **one** test robot. Keep the old map on the robot under its own name so you can switch back.
3. Relocalise the robot and confirm its confidence is normal at the start position.
4. In DRISHTI Map, click the robot. Check that **current map** is the candidate and the checksum (md5) matches the one in the report. If it does not match, the robot is not running your file. Stop and find out why.
5. Point at the robot and press **rec** to start a recording.
6. Drive the robot through the old trouble spot and around the whole moved piece. Drive both directions if robots do.
7. At every action point, charger and parking position inside the moved piece, let the robot dock once and look at it: is it where it used to be on the floor?
8. Stop the recording. Press **Recording summary**: no jump, confidence normal, no abnormal localisation state. Press **Detect with Recording** with **Map** = the candidate: no fault.
9. Only then roll the map out to the other robots, the way your site does it. Afterwards run [[WI-07]] to confirm every robot has the same file.

## If something is wrong in the trial

| What happened | What to do |
|---|---|
| Robot stops with a virtual-laser fault | A feature line is in its way. Run **Detect**, remove the line ([[WI-06]]), rebuild, repeat from step 1. |
| Jump at a new place | The region cut is wrong or the piece is larger than the region. Back to [[WI-02]] with the trial recording. |
| Docking is off at one station | Move that station by hand ([[WI-06]]) by the amount you measure on the floor. |
| Low confidence where bins or pallets stand | Normal: movable things are not map walls. Check that the robot keeps its position through it. |

## Before any new build

Somebody may have edited the map on the robot during the trial. Download the robot's current map again ([[WI-01]] C) and build from that file, not from yours. Press **Compare** between your file and the robot's to see what changed.
