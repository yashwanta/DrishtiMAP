# WI-02 · Find out why robots jump

**Purpose:** find where the map disagrees with the building, and whether the cause is a piece of map in the wrong place.

**You need:** the map the robots use ([[WI-01]] C) and a recording through the trouble spot ([[WI-01]] D).

## Steps

1. On the **Tools** tab choose the **Recording** and the **Map**.
2. Press **Detect with Recording**. The first run takes a minute or two because the program rebuilds the drive from the lasers; later runs are quick.
3. Read the result at the bottom and look at the map:

| You see | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Red circle, `JUMP 1.36 m` | The map steps by that much here. A robot driving here jumps. |
| Yellow circle, `step 0.3 m` | A smaller step. Worth a look, not counted as a jump. |
| `(ambiguous)` | The place looks the same in two positions (a long plain aisle). The step proves nothing. |
| `(track restart)` | The robot was relocalised or samples are missing. Not a map fault. |
| `does not fit this map as one rigid track` | Part of the map is misplaced even if no single step reaches 0.5 m. |
| Red diamond, `line on path` | A feature line lies in the robot's way. See [[KB-06]] and [[WI-06]]. |
| Yellow diamond, `low wall match` | Few laser points land on map walls. Robots report low confidence here. |

4. If there is a jump or the drive is not rigid, press **Find rigid pieces**. It cuts the drive into stretches over which the map is rigid and gives, for each seam, the **step** and the **turn** against the stretch before.

5. Read the pieces:

| Pattern | Cause |
|---|---|
| Two long pieces, a turn of 1 to 3 degrees between them, a step of a metre or more at the seam | One piece of the map is attached at the wrong angle. Go to [[WI-03]]. |
| Two long pieces, no turn, a step at the seam | One piece is shifted. Go to [[WI-03]]; the measured turn will be near zero. |
| Many short pieces all over | The recording is poor, or the area has changed a lot. Record again, or rescan the area. |
| One piece, low wall match in one spot | Walls there are missing, moved or doubled. Compare with an older map ([[WI-06]], Take walls). |

6. Write down the **sample ranges** of the misplaced piece and of a piece that is right. You need them in [[WI-03]].

## Check your reading

Press **Show drift** with the reference samples filled in (section 3 of the Tools tab). The list shows how far the map departs from the drive along the route. A difference that is near zero at one end and grows steadily with distance is a piece attached at an angle: the error is small near the pivot and large far from it.

## Do not

- Do not fix the place where the jump shows. The jump shows at the seam; the cause is the whole piece behind it.
- Do not rescan the seam area only. A rescan laid on a misplaced piece inherits its error. See [[KB-03]].
